Announcements

Announcement: Transition to Monthly Publication Model

Published: December 2026 | Editorial Office

Dear Authors, Reviewers, and Readers,

To accelerate the dissemination of scientific knowledge and enhance the global visibility of accepted research, the Journal of Jilin University is pleased to announce a major transition in its editorial workflow.

As of December 2026, our journal has officially adopted the Monthly Publication Model.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS OF THE MONTHLY MODEL:

  • Regular Monthly Releases: Articles are assigned and published in the active issue of the corresponding month upon peer-review completion.
  • Streamlined Editorial Workflows: Eliminates manuscript backlog and layout bottlenecks, significantly reducing turnaround time.
  • Immediate DOI & Citation: Published papers receive complete volume, issue, and DOI metadata without waiting for seasonal cycles.

We invite researchers and academics to submit their manuscripts to take advantage of our streamlined and accelerated monthly publication pipeline.

Ethics Policy

Generative AI Policy

The implementation of these policies stems from the growing impact of generative artificial intelligence (AI) and AI-assisted technologies. The purpose of these policies is to enhance transparency and provide clear guidance for all contributors.

  • Acceptable Use: Improving language, grammar, visual creation (with disclosure), and initial outline drafting.
  • Prohibited Use: Generating core scientific content, analysis, results, or conclusions via AI is strictly prohibited.
  • Disclosure: Authors must explicitly declare AI usage (e.g., "Language and grammar structures were improved using ChatGPT (OpenAI, 2025).").
  • Authorship: AI tools cannot be credited as authors.
  • Reviewers & Editors: Must not upload confidential manuscripts into public AI tools. AI platforms cannot replace professional peer evaluation or editorial decision-making.

Plagiarism Policy

Plagiarism includes direct copying without citation, substantial paraphrasing without reference, self-plagiarism (duplicate publication), and data plagiarism. All submissions are screened using specialized plagiarism detection software. Manuscripts exceeding acceptable similarity thresholds will be directly rejected.

Post-Publication Corrections

In accordance with COPE guidelines, the journal may publish Corrections, Retractions, or Expressions of Concern. Retracted articles remain accessible in the record to preserve transparency but are prominently flagged.

Informed Consent

For research involving human participants, formal informed consent must be documented prior to data collection. Participant anonymity, confidentiality, and voluntary participation must be protected at all times.

Submission & Review Guidelines

Submission Method

Please submit your manuscript files directly to the official editorial inbox: editor@jilinjournal.org

Originality and Submission Conditions

Manuscripts submitted to the journal must be original, must not have been previously published, and must not be under consideration for publication elsewhere. Submissions that do not meet these conditions will not be reviewed.

All studies undergo a preliminary editorial screening for scope compatibility, originality, academic quality, ethical compliance, and adherence to formatting guidelines. Articles may be rejected without peer review by the editor-in-chief if they fail to comply with instructions or fall outside the scope.

File Formats & Similarity Guidelines

Articles must be submitted as Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) documents. The recommended total similarity rate should not exceed 20% (with bibliography excluded), and the similarity rate from any single source should ideally remain below 1%.

For publications generated from graduate theses, similar threshold guidelines apply. Legal liability arising from plagiarism rests entirely with the author(s).

Peer Review Process (Double-Blind)

  • 1. Preliminary Editorial Evaluation: Evaluated by the editorial team for scope alignment, author guidelines adherence, originality, and ethical standards.
  • 2. Double-Blind Peer Review: Accepted preliminary works are sent to at least two independent expert reviewers. Both author and reviewer identities remain strictly confidential throughout.
  • 3. Evaluation Criteria: Originality and contribution to the field, theoretical and methodological rigor, clarity of presentation and structure, alignment with journal aims and scope, and compliance with ethical rules.
  • 4. Editorial Decision: Acceptance, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Comprehensive Resubmission, or Rejection.
  • 5. Revision Process: Authors must respond in detail to reviewer comments and provide a "response to reviewers" file along with the revised text.
  • 6. Ethical Oversight & Confidentiality: Conducted in accordance with COPE guidelines.

Writing Rules

Manuscript texts must be written in “MS Word” using Times New Roman font, with no indentation (space) at the beginning of paragraphs. Numbering must not be used in section headings or sub-headings; the first letter of each word must be capitalized, and headings must be 11 pt, bold, and left-aligned. Paragraph spacing must be 6 pt before and after, with single line spacing. All margins must be 2 cm.

The first letters of table, figure, and chart captions must be capitalized; captions must be 10 pt, bold, and left-aligned. Captions must appear above the tables, charts, and figures. Tables, charts, and figures must be left-aligned. If there is a source note, it must appear beneath the table, chart, or figure, and must be 10 pt, bold, and left-aligned. In tables, only horizontal lines must be used. Vertical lines must not be used. The font size must be 10 pt, the paragraph spacing must be 0 pt before and 0 pt after, and the line spacing must be single.

References

In the references section, the paragraph spacing must be 6 pt before and 6 pt after, the line spacing must be single, and the special indentation must be set to “hanging” by 1 cm. The references section of manuscripts must be prepared in accordance with the APA 7 reference formatting rules.

Copyright & Legal Responsibility

The responsibility for articles published in the journal rests entirely with the authors. Upon submission of the manuscript, the copyright is transferred to the journal, and authors waive any rights to claim ownership over submitted works or pursue legal action regarding the publication process.
